Pace zones

From a 4:35/km marathon. On hills, run the effort.

Each run is a range. The low end is the session and the top is optional, so stop at the low end if your calf feels tight. A run is Hit when you cover at least 90% of the low end, stay within 10% of the top, and average within 5 s/km of the zone. On LT and race days only distance is checked, so look at your laps in Strava for the LT block. Wednesdays are PT.
