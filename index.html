Pace band

Built on a 4:35/km marathon pace. On hills, run the effort and let the pace drift.

Wednesdays are PT. A run counts as hit when you cover at least 90% of the distance and average within 5 s/km of the band. On LT days only distance is checked, so look at your laps in Strava for the LT block.
